About Tap Drill Calculation

Thread Systems

  • ISO Metric (M): International standard, measured in millimeters
  • American National (UNC/UNF): Unified thread standard, measured in inches
  • BSW: British Standard Whitworth, 55° thread angle
  • Acme: Trapezoidal thread, 29° thread angle

Thread Percentage Guide

  • 75%: Standard for most applications
  • 70%: Tougher materials, reduces tap breakage
  • 60%: Very tough materials (stainless steel)
  • 80-85%: Soft materials (aluminum, brass)
